# FGCU Foundation Scholarship Application — verification snapshot

Source: https://www.fgcu.edu/admissionsandaid/financialaid/undergraduate/typesofaid/fgcufoundationscholarships
Snapshot date: 2026-07-28

Florida Gulf Coast University Foundation scholarships support FGCU students through donor-funded awards.

Current cycle status:
- The 2026-2027 application period is closed.
- Dates for the 2027-2028 application period are expected to be published in Fall 2026.

Who should apply:
- Incoming freshmen and transfer students who have applied to FGCU.
- Currently enrolled undergraduate FGCU students.
- Currently enrolled graduate FGCU students.

Selection context:
- Foundation scholarships include awards based on academic merit.
- Some awards are based on financial need.
- Individual awards may also use donor- or department-specific criteria such as major of study or other specifications.

Application context:
- Students use the FGCU Foundation Scholarship application.
- A University Identification Number (UIN) is required to submit the application.
- FAFSA is required for consideration for need-based Foundation scholarships.
- Essays are an important part of the selection process.
