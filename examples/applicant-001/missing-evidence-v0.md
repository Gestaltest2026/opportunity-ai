# User #1 Missing Evidence v0

Purpose: prevent Opportunity AI from converting unknown applicant facts into scholarship eligibility claims.

## Blocking eligibility facts

These must remain `UNKNOWN` until User #1 or an official record confirms them.

1. **FGCU transfer GPA**
   - Needed for FGCU transfer/admissions scholarships and merit thresholds.

2. **AA degree / Florida college transfer status**
   - Needed for transfer scholarship rules that require an AA degree from a Florida college.

3. **Florida residency for tuition / aid purposes**
   - Needed for Florida state aid and some FGCU scholarships or waivers.

4. **FAFSA completion and aid year**
   - Needed for FGCU Foundation Scholarships, state aid, and need-based programs.

5. **Financial need / income threshold / SAI or Pell-related status**
   - Needed for Patsy Mink, Jeannette Rankin, P.E.O. PCE, FSAG-type sources, and other need-based opportunities.

6. **First bachelor's degree status**
   - Needed for Jeannette Rankin and many nontraditional/adult learner grants.

7. **2026-27 enrollment status and course load**
   - Needed for full-time/part-time eligibility distinctions.

8. **U.S. citizenship, permanent residency, or eligible noncitizen status**
   - Needed for many federal/state and private programs.

9. **Current dependent-child / mother eligibility language**
   - User #1 has confirmed mother evidence, but each funder may define dependent children, custody, or support differently.

10. **Resume and updated personal statement**
    - Needed to strengthen evidence for legal work, community service, hula leadership, caregiving, and professional-to-academic transition.

## Nonblocking but high-value evidence

- FGCU student portal scholarship status screenshot or award notice.
- Transcript or unofficial academic record.
- Current Legal Studies program requirements / expected graduation confirmation.
- Law office role description or reference letter.
- Hula school/community service documentation.
- Caregiving constraints only if User #1 chooses to disclose them.

## Guardrail

If a source requires a fact listed above, Opportunity AI may mark the source as `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`, but must not mark it as `APPLICATION_READY` until the missing fact is confirmed.
