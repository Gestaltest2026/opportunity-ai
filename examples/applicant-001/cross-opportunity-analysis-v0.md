# User #1 Cross-Opportunity Analysis v0

## Purpose

Test whether Opportunity-specific Representation changes anything substantive beyond wording.

## Result

Representation changed **application strategy and the highest-value missing fact**, but did **not yet change the current pursue judgment**, because all three cases remain conditional for different operational reasons.

This is a partial validation of the Representation thesis, not a full validation.

## Invariant applicant facts

Across the three opportunities, several facts remain stable:

- Current FGCU Legal Studies transfer student
- Long-term community service since 2007
- Legal Assistant → Paralegal → Office Manager progression
- Law-school / public-contribution career direction

These are canonical Applicant facts and must not change by Opportunity.

## Primary↔deprioritized switches

Observed switches demonstrate that the layer is doing more than copy editing:

- English Literature study: **deprioritized** for the Out-of-State Waiver → **primary** for Perez Study Abroad.
- Leadership/community service: **primary** for the Out-of-State Waiver → **supporting** for Foundation and Perez.
- Caregiving: **primary** for Foundation → not foregrounded for the waiver or Perez.
- International adaptation / ESOL: **primary** for Perez → not foregrounded for the waiver.

## Effect on decision

Current pursue judgments:
- Foundation: conditional
- Out-of-State Waiver: conditional
- Perez Study Abroad: conditional

Therefore, there is **no observed pursue-judgment change yet**.

What did change materially:
1. the strongest reason to pursue,
2. the decisive missing fact,
3. the evidence to foreground,
4. the essay/application strategy if the opportunity becomes actionable.

## Interpretation

The current evidence supports this narrower claim:

> Opportunity-specific Representation can materially change application strategy and information priorities while preserving a stable canonical Applicant.

It does **not yet support** the stronger claim:

> Opportunity-specific Representation changes which opportunity a human should pursue.

That stronger claim requires at least one pair where eligibility/actionability is comparable enough that narrative/evidence representation can actually move the pursue decision or ranking.

## Next validation requirement

Select at least two simultaneously actionable opportunities for which the applicant plausibly satisfies hard eligibility. Then compare whether Opportunity-specific Representation changes:

- pursue vs do-not-pursue,
- relative priority,
- expected application effort,
- evidence/essay strategy.

Until that comparison exists, Narrative Fit should remain **partially observed**, not fully validated.
