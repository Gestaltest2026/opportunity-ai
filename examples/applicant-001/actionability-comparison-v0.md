# Applicant 001 — Actionability Comparison v0

## Purpose

This document separates **representation quality** from **current actionability**. The previous three-opportunity comparison showed meaningful narrative differences, but all pursue judgments remained conditional because at least one operational gate was unresolved or the cycle was closed.

The next validation requires at least two opportunities that are simultaneously comparable on actionability. We will not fabricate that condition when the live market does not provide it.

## Current live-market observation — 2026-07-29

### FGCU Foundation Scholarships
- Official 2026–27 cycle: closed.
- 2027–28 dates are expected in Fall 2026.
- Use: next-cycle candidate, not currently actionable.

### FGCU Education Abroad Scholarships
- Several named 2026 awards have already passed their application deadlines.
- Some programs/grants may remain structurally relevant, but actionability depends on current study-abroad participation and program-specific timing.
- Use: conditional / program-dependent candidate, not assumed actionable.

### FGCU Foundation application structure
- The official application includes donor-matching dimensions such as major, career interest, research, affiliations, and other attributes.
- This is evidence that a single institutional application can route one Applicant into multiple donor-specific opportunities, making Opportunity-specific Representation useful at the sub-award level.

## Decision

Do **not** claim that Narrative Fit has changed opportunity ranking yet.

Before ranking validation, build a live comparison set with:
1. at least two opportunities open at the same time;
2. hard eligibility substantially confirmed;
3. materially different selection preferences;
4. meaningful application burden;
5. human pursue judgment recorded before AI ranking.

## Two-lane sourcing strategy

### Lane 1 — Current actionability
Search beyond FGCU-only sources for Florida-wide and external scholarships that are open now and plausibly available to Applicant 001.

### Lane 2 — Next-cycle institutional fit
Track FGCU Foundation 2027–28 reopening and donor-level matching criteria so the system can test Representation quality against real sub-awards when the cycle opens.

## Guardrail

A closed, expired, or qualification-dependent opportunity may remain useful as a representation fixture, but it must not be labeled as a live pursue candidate.
