# User #1 Representation Experiment — Real Opportunities

These three cases are selected from official FGCU sources to test whether one canonical Applicant produces materially different, evidence-grounded representations under different selection logics.

## Opportunity A — FGCU Foundation Scholarships

- opportunity_id: `opportunity-001`
- official source: https://www.fgcu.edu/admissionsandaid/financialaid/undergraduate/typesofaid/fgcufoundationscholarships
- role in experiment: broad institutional / donor-fit case
- current status: existing ground-truth fixture; current cycle closed
- why selected: broad scholarship pool where academic persistence, caregiving, community contribution, career direction, and donor-specific preferences may matter depending on the individual award.
- representation hypothesis: foreground persistence, nontraditional educational trajectory, legal-career direction, caregiving, and community contribution only where supported by the specific award.

## Opportunity B — FGCU Out-of-State Waiver

- opportunity_id: `opportunity-003`
- official source: https://www.fgcu.edu/admissionsandaid/undergraduateadmissions/scholarshipsandwaivers
- role in experiment: admissions merit + leadership / civic-engagement case
- current status: source verified; applicant eligibility unresolved because Florida residency classification and transfer GPA are not confirmed in the canonical Applicant Profile.
- hard gate signals from official source:
  - classified as non-Florida resident for tuition purposes
  - published application deadline
  - priority to newly admitted transfer students
  - minimum transfer GPA 3.4
- selection signals from official source:
  - applicants ranked by GPA
  - committee may consider outstanding leadership
  - civic engagement
  - demonstrated talents
  - other activities critical to FGCU mission
- application narrative signals:
  - scholastic achievements / community awards / leadership / volunteer activity
  - future plans and career goals
  - extraordinary challenges or personal circumstances
- representation hypothesis: foreground founder/instructor leadership, long-term community service, legal-career progression, and extraordinary life circumstances; do not treat these as substitutes for the residency/GPA gates.

## Opportunity C — Perez Family Study Abroad Scholarship

- opportunity_id: `opportunity-004`
- official source: https://www.fgcu.edu/academics/studyabroad/scholarships
- role in experiment: academic/career coherence + international-development case
- current status: official source verified; applicant eligibility unresolved because study-abroad program participation, GPA, financial need, and remaining FGCU coursework are not confirmed.
- hard gate / qualification signals from official source:
  - currently enrolled full- or part-time degree-seeking undergraduate
  - financial need
  - study-abroad credits apply toward degree completion
  - cumulative GPA 2.8 or higher, subject to committee discretion
  - admitted to or applying for an FGCU-approved study-abroad program
  - at least one full semester of FGCU coursework remaining after study abroad
- selection signals from official source:
  - maturity to benefit from the experience
  - coherent link between proposed study-abroad activities and future academic/career plans
  - personal development goals
  - community service
  - leadership/campus/community involvement desirable
- representation hypothesis: foreground Japan-to-Florida lived experience, ESOL/English-literature study, legal-studies trajectory, future law-school direction, maturity, and community service; this representation should differ materially from Opportunity B's merit/leadership framing.

## Experiment rule

These cases are not treated as equally actionable. Eligibility uncertainty is part of the test. The representation layer may change fit or application strategy, but it must never override hard eligibility gates.
